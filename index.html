For Faculty & PIs

        
As a principal investigator, you get direct access to national resources and can sponsor your entire lab team — grad students, postdocs, and RAs — at no cost.

        

          
1
Create Your Alliance Account
Register at ccdb.alliancecan.ca with your @uwindsor.ca email. Approval takes 1–2 business days.

          
2
Consult Paul Preney
Book a free session with Windsor's on-campus SHARCNET consultant. He'll match your workflow to the right cluster and help you get running fast.

          
3
Apply for Resource Allocation
Submit a RAC (Resource Allocation Competition) proposal for large-scale jobs. Paul can help write the technical section. Annual deadline: November.

          
4
Sponsor Your Team
Add your grad students and RAs to your CCDB account. They get the same free national access under your PI umbrella.

        

      

      

        
What you get

        
80,000+ CPU cores

        
A100/H100 GPU nodes

        
Project storage (team shared)

        
DMP tools for grant proposals

        
Priority RAC allocations

        
Sponsor up to 10+ lab members

      

    


    
    

      

        
For Graduate Students

        
Get access to the same resources world-class research labs use — through your supervisor's account or directly if you're a PhD student.

        

          
1
Get Sponsored by Your PI
Ask your supervisor to add you as a sponsored user in their CCDB account at ccdb.alliancecan.ca.

          
2
Take Free HPC Training
SHARCNET runs free online courses on HPC basics, Python on clusters, GPU computing, and SLURM. See upcoming events →

          
3
Submit Your First Job
SSH into narval.alliancecan.ca, write a SLURM script, submit with sbatch.

          
4
Manage & Share Data
Use Nextcloud for lab file sharing, Globus for large transfers, and FRDR to publish your thesis data with a DOI.

        

      

      

        
What you get

        
Shared cluster CPU & GPU access

        
50GB Nextcloud storage

        
Full software stack (Lmod)

        
Free HPC training courses

        
Direct support from Paul Preney

      

    


    
    

      

        
For Undergrads & RAs

        
Working on a research project? Your faculty advisor can give you access to the same national supercomputers as their grad students.

        

          
1
Talk to Your Supervisor
Your faculty advisor needs to sponsor you in their CCDB account. Reach out to them or contact Paul Preney for guidance.

          
2
Take the Intro Course
SHARCNET's "Introduction to HPC" is free, online, and takes ~2 hours. Linux basics, SSH, and job submission. Register →

          
3
Start with the Debug Queue
Use --partition=debug for short test runs (<1h). Move to the regular queue once your jobs are working.

        

      

      

        
What you get

        
Access under PI's allocation

        
Basic Nextcloud file access

        
Full Lmod software stack

        
Free consultation with Paul
